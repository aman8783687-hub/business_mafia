# Analytics and growth

The pipeline has no YouTube data, and the agent never invents performance numbers. Operator notes and readings of YouTube Studio (logged in `reports/analytics.md`) are the only signal.

## What the data says so far (`reports/analytics.md`)

- Episode 1 (petrol pump, day 1): retention strong (average view duration 2:00 of 3:19), 43% of views from search, but thumbnail CTR only 2.6% on 117 impressions. The click is the bottleneck, not the story.
- The operator's own hand-made thumbnail (question title in brush bands, curious boss, money) replaced the old "हिसाब" infographic; the template now copies it. Title moved to "<X> वाला कितना कमाता है? 💰 | <hook>".
- Read Studio again after 48 hours and 7 days per episode and add a row; change one packaging variable at a time.

## What the agent can do

- Choose topics per `topic-strategy.md` with real search demand.
- Make the first 20 seconds earn the rest (`script-formula.md`): retention is the lever.
- Change one variable at a time when trying something new; log it in `reports/experiments.md`.

## For the operator (outside the pipeline)

Post on a fixed schedule at 6-9 PM IST; cut a Short from `shorts_hook`; add every episode to its vertical's playlist; pin the comment early; post the community poll; reply to early comments; after 48 hours, if CTR is low, try a `title_alternates` entry via Test & Compare.
